# AI-Powered Business Intelligence & Decision Support Platform

## 1. Project Overview

The AI-Powered Business Intelligence & Decision Support Platform is an end-to-end data analytics and AI-based business intelligence system.

The platform transforms retail transaction data into actionable business insights using data cleaning, customer analytics, RFM analysis, customer segmentation, risk prediction, decision-support logic, and interactive Power BI dashboards.

## 2. Problem Statement

Businesses generate large amounts of transactional data, but raw data alone does not provide clear information about customer behavior, product performance, or customer risk.

This project aims to transform raw retail data into meaningful insights and support business decision-making through analytics and AI-based risk prediction.

## 3. Objectives

- Clean and preprocess retail transaction data
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior
- Perform RFM analysis
- Segment customers based on behavior
- Identify customer risk
- Generate customer-level recommendations
- Analyze product and revenue performance
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
- Provide business decision support

## 6. Key Analysis

### RFM Analysis

The project analyzes customers using:

- Recency
- Frequency
- Monetary Value

These measures are used to understand customer purchasing behavior and create customer segments.

### Customer Segmentation

Customers are grouped according to their purchasing behavior and value.

### Risk Prediction

The project identifies customers according to their risk level and calculates risk probability.

### Decision Support

The system converts customer analysis and risk results into actionable recommendations.
